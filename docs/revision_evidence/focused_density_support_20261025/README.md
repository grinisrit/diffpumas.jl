# Matched finite-budget rock-density and support stress

The v2 outputs are the authoritative five-arm producer results. Independent initial/final full forward/Jacobian, objective/profile/branch-gradient and field-summary reviews passed; their records are under `focused_revision_20261025/density_independent_run` and the associated density review JSON files. This is a bounded sensitivity investigation, not a search for converged minima or calibrated geological uncertainty. The original primary field and production physics/correction artifacts are unchanged.

The first v1 baseline/rho2760 jobs failed before any initial checkpoint or optimisation update: a Julia nested-function callback captured the outer ray-cache binding. Their exact source (`failed_driver_v1_snapshot.jl` and unchanged `dev/focused_density_support.jl`), execution logs and failed end statuses remain under `arms/`. Neither failure is interpreted as a scientific outcome. The corrected v2 source changes only the conflicting callback names; fresh results live under `arms_v2/`. No v1 result is substituted into the final comparisons. The production-GN/deadline regression passed 18 checks; the actual-v2-function scope regression passed 3 checks.

All five arms start from the **original primary** field, not a polished endpoint. Fixed inputs are the original 600-cell geometry and 1003 covered paths, original 690-bin fitted mask and observations, 32 midpoint energy nodes, fixed dry/material correction, 0.3 robust log scale, 0.15 DEM slant weights, profiled normalisation,absolute graph weight 3.954930197858131 and [0,0.9] bounds. Density arms change bulk rock density consistently in every rock/water segment, remaining dry rock and column-mass denominator, while retaining mass stopping powers/composition and calibration coefficients. Geometry is always built at the original 2710 density. Support arms impose 400 or 800m height cutoffs; these discrete stresses change actual grid membership, unlike nearby cutoffs that could select identical cells. Unsupported cells remain zero; expanded support starts at zero and removed cells are zeroed.

Initial effects are separated explicitly in `initial_effects.toml`: (1) original primary physics/field/support, (2) arm density with the original field/support, (3) projected arm initial field/support. Subsequent accepted updates are optimisation effects. All costs retain data/prior decomposition and profile normalisation. Initial scalar/full/cache primal values, original baseline flux/objective/profile/branch gradient and 500m support reproduce exactly. Positive-length geometric crossing is checked against the original dry covered-Jacobian nonzero selection.

The numerical protocol is unchanged production active-set GN: at most 10 outer attempts (hence at most 10 accepts),six LM searches with 14 step halvings and a seventh gradient safeguard with 40 halvings, original damping and projected Armijo rule. The soft budget is 900 s per arm, checked before/after trial and full-confirmation work; candidates crossing it are not accepted. Only initial or full-confirmed states can be checkpointed. The external 1050 s cap preserves incomplete status on a hard kill rather than fabricating a final endpoint. All v2 arms ended cleanly; the baseline stopped after all directions were rejected, and the other four reached their ten-attempt budgets. None met the unchanged selected-branch tolerance 1e-6; no generalised stationarity is certified.

`summary.json` compares only within-arm cost decreases and reports common observed residual/flux responses against the matched baseline continuation. Absolute objectives from changed density models/support constraints must not be ranked as minima. Region summaries give unweighted cell mass-fraction means/counts on current, original, intersection,union,new and removed supports, plus prior-only cells and six fixed grid-height planes. These are effective replacement fractions, not porosity, storage or volume-weighted inventories. Changing the mask changes the averaging region, so current-support means alone are not comparable physical inventories.

Every arm retains all 600-cell fields, all 1003 predictions with the 690-bin mask, initial/final log residuals, every trial/rejection reason, each full-confirmed checkpoint, source/runtime/output hashes and exact argv. `plan.toml` and `commands_v2.json` bind the experimental definitions; the collector is a separate saved-output-only calculation. Producer inputs remained immutable during execution. Independent review records are maintained separately under `focused_revision_20261025`.

The global `*.log` ignore rule also matches these small curated execution/test logs. They are required diagnostic evidence and must be explicitly force-added if retained in Git; `required_ignored_logs.txt` lists the exact paths. Large generated example trees and the externally hash-bound physics dump are not copied here.
