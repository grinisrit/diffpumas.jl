# Final independent material correction validation

Completed 2026-10-24 with unchanged recorded source/runtime/input hashes and all raw output hashes independently verified. Training uses 8,192 MC energy histories per field/direction; final testing uses 1,024 histories in eight independent replicate streams. The final coefficients (linear -0.5903848352831378; quadratic 0.5777418379132017) are frozen before any test evaluation. The ridge, splits, fields and acceptance rules were not retuned after the pilot.

The fixed historical dry correction is retained. Two material coefficients fit paired dry/wet log-flux contrasts at uniform mass fractions 0.2 and 0.6 on 12 training directions. Twelve test directions have disjoint azimuths and include 60/75-degree angular extrapolation; test fields are uniform 0.35, two unseen spatial patterns and a localized 0.6 lens with zeros elsewhere. Exact pilot/production training/test energy and transport seed-set intersections are empty. Field/replicate pairings retain covariance. Training column fractions span 0.2--0.6; 12 of 48 held-out ray/field cases fall below 0.2, including three exactly dry columns.

## Prespecified selected-case acceptance

Aggregate median absolute relative flux bias decreases 24.983% to 12.937% (48.2% reduction, exceeding the 30% improvement threshold). The maximum stays 59.936% on a dry-column ray where the material multiplier equals one. Every test-field flux median improves (43--53%). The localized median improves 22.949% to 13.060%.

Matched-energy finite-contrast derivative median absolute relative bias decreases 25.254% to 21.373%; comparisons exceeding twice their conditional replicate standard error decrease 16 to 6 of 48, with no newly exceeding comparison. All four field medians and counts improve; the localized median decreases 28.719% to 25.554%, with count 2 to 1 of 12. Maximum AD versus same-surrogate finite-difference relative error is 1.287e-8, below the 1e-4 consistency threshold. Three all-dry selected cases use one-sided 0--0.1 MC secants and second-order one-sided local deterministic differences.

These aggregate selected-case rules pass. This is conditional average improvement, not universal agreement: 43/48 flux residual means still exceed twice their conditional standard error, the 75-degree median flux bias remains 26.86%, and the 15-degree derivative median worsens 10.92% to 22.55% even while its >2-SE count decreases 3 to 1. Selected derivatives use the largest sensitivity among crossed interior wet cells when available, otherwise the largest crossed sensitivity; this does not validate the near-zero sensitivity population. Relative errors become unstable near zero MC secants.

`paired_assessment.json` is bound to raw data and its helper by `derived_provenance.json`. `coverage_assessment.json` adds field/zenith detail with independent `coverage_provenance.json`. Its optional jackknife gain flags are descriptive only: nonsmooth absolute bias and uncertain bias sign can yield degenerate jackknife error estimates; the flags are not significance or acceptance tests. Raw paired conditional standard errors omit fitted-coefficient uncertainty and cross-bin covariance. Eight replicates do not calibrate confidence coverage.

Transport remains stochastic energy loss on fixed straight paths with scattering disabled. The 30 km primary source altitude is held unchanged for attribution and is not independently physically resolved here. Neither this validation nor the same-reference historical replay establishes water-recovery accuracy.
